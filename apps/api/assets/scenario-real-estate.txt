Metro Properties — Property Operations Handbook

SECTION 1: PORTFOLIO OVERVIEW

Metro Properties manages residential and mixed-use buildings across three
communities: Dubai Marina, Business Bay, and Downtown Dubai. Each building
has an on-site facilities team and a shared pool of approved vendors for
work that falls outside what the on-site team can handle directly. This
handbook is the reference facilities staff and the tenant-relations team
use to decide how a maintenance request should be routed and resolved.

SECTION 2: MAINTENANCE REQUEST INTAKE

A tenant maintenance request must include: the unit number, a description
of the issue, and — for anything involving water, electricity, or gas —
whether the issue is currently active (ongoing leak, power out) or
resolved but needs repair (a leak that has stopped, a socket that no
longer works). Active issues involving water or gas are treated as
emergencies regardless of time of day; resolved issues are scheduled
during standard business hours. A request missing the unit number or a
usable description is returned to the tenant for clarification before it
is logged, because an unclear request cannot be routed to the right
vendor category.

SECTION 3: MAINTENANCE ESCALATION PROCESS

Every logged request is first assessed by the on-site facilities team to
determine whether it can be resolved in-house or requires a specialist
vendor. In-house resolution covers minor items: a stuck door, a loose
fixture, a clogged drain that responds to standard clearing. If the
on-site team cannot resolve the issue within one visit, or the issue falls
into a specialist category (HVAC, elevator, structural, pest control), the
request is escalated to the matching approved vendor from Section 4.

An escalated request that is not acknowledged by the assigned vendor within
2 hours (for emergencies) or 1 business day (for scheduled work) is
automatically re-escalated to the property manager, who selects a backup
vendor from the same category. This exists because a single unresponsive
vendor should never be allowed to stall an emergency indefinitely.

SECTION 4: APPROVED VENDORS BY CATEGORY

HVAC emergencies (no cooling, no heating, unusual noise or smell from a
unit) are handled exclusively by Gulf Climate Services, the building's
contracted HVAC vendor, who commits to a 2-hour response window for
emergencies and next-business-day for non-emergency HVAC requests.
Elevator issues are handled by Skyline Elevator Co. under a maintenance
contract that includes mandatory same-day response for any elevator
reported stuck with a passenger inside. Pest control is handled by
GreenGuard Pest Solutions on a scheduled quarterly basis per building, plus
on-demand callouts for active infestations. Plumbing and electrical work
beyond what the on-site team can resolve is handled by a rotating panel of
three licensed contractors, assigned by whichever has current availability
at the time of escalation.

SECTION 5: BUILDING POLICIES

Common-area renovations or unit-interior work that could affect building
systems (plumbing, electrical, load-bearing walls) require written
approval from the property manager before work begins, along with proof
that the tenant's contractor carries liability insurance. Noise-generating
work (drilling, hammering) is restricted to 9:00 AM–6:00 PM on weekdays
and 10:00 AM–4:00 PM on Saturdays, with no exceptions during the two weeks
before and after major public holidays, when buildings receive the highest
volume of unrelated noise complaints.

SECTION 6: LEASE AND SERVICE DOCUMENTATION

Every lease includes a service-level appendix specifying which
maintenance categories are covered by the building's service charge
(structural, common-area, building systems) versus which are the tenant's
own responsibility (in-unit appliances not provided by the building,
cosmetic damage caused by the tenant). A maintenance request for an
item the tenant is responsible for is still logged and routed normally,
but the resulting invoice — if a vendor visit is required — is billed to
the tenant rather than the building's maintenance budget.

SECTION 7: VENDOR CONTRACT TERMS

All approved vendors operate under a standard contract requiring: current
trade license and insurance on file with the property manager, a defined
response-time commitment per request category (see Section 4), and
quarterly performance review against that commitment. A vendor that
misses its committed response time on more than 15% of requests in a
quarter is placed on a corrective action plan; a second consecutive
quarter of missed targets is grounds for removal from the approved vendor
list.
